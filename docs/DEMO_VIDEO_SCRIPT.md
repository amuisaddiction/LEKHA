# LEKHA — Official Demo Video Script & Shot List

**Target Duration:** 2 minutes to 2 minutes 30 seconds  
**Upload Requirement:** Public YouTube video (or Vimeo)  
**Tone:** Calm, intelligent, editorial, professional, confident

---

## Shot List & Narrative Timeline

### Scene 1: The Problem (0:00 – 0:30)
- **Visual:** Screen opens on LEKHA homepage hero with editorial typography ("Your skin has a story. Let's keep track.") and the brand tagline.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Every day, millions of people scan their skin using beauty apps. But most of those apps do two things: they flash an arbitrary score that creates anxiety, and they ignore the fact that skin changes with lighting, sleep, and camera conditions. When people try to track their progress, they're left guessing whether their skincare routine is actually working—or if the lighting was just different."

### Scene 2: The LEKHA Philosophy & Solution (0:30 – 0:50)
- **Visual:** Scroll smoothly through the "Our Point of View" manifesto and the 3-step practice.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "This is LEKHA—a skin intelligence platform and personal skin record built for the Perfect Corp YouCam Hackathon. LEKHA stands for honest documentation. We connect directly to the certified YouCam Skin AI API, but we give users the context to understand their results without misleading them."

### Scene 3: Capture & Pre-Flight Skin Truth Check (0:50 – 1:20)
- **Visual:** Click "Begin Scan" or "New Scan". Drag and drop a clear portrait photo. Show the live **Skin Truth Check** dashboard updating in real time with Lighting, Clarity, and Resolution badges.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Before any image is submitted, LEKHA runs a client-side Skin Truth Check. It analyzes ambient exposure, edge sharpness, and camera resolution. This ensures the user gets accurate diagnostic results and understands their image conditions upfront."

### Scene 4: Certified YouCam Skin AI Integration (1:20 – 1:45)
- **Visual:** Click "Execute Genuine Skin Analysis". Show the genuine loading sequence contacting the YouCam S2S gateway. Transition to the Results View. Point out the UI Score vs Raw Score labels and click a detection mask overlay.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Our backend securely stages the image with YouCam's File API, creates the diagnostic task, and polls for certified results. Look at the results screen: for every metric—from texture and pores to redness and moisture—we display both the consumer-calibrated UI Score and the continuous scientific Raw Model Output. Users can even toggle authentic detection mask overlays directly on their portrait."

### Scene 5: Skin Passport & Scan Comparison with Comparison Confidence (1:45 – 2:15)
- **Visual:** Click "Save to Passport". Switch to the **Skin Passport** view showing dated scan records. Click "Compare Scans". Select two scans and highlight the **Comparison Confidence** panel.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Every completed scan is stored in the user's private browser Skin Passport. But here is LEKHA's core differentiator: when comparing two scans over time, our Comparison Confidence engine evaluates whether differences in lighting or camera resolution make the comparison less reliable. We never pretend that every numerical change is biological progress."

### Scene 6: Skincare Guidance, Privacy & Conclusion (2:15 – 2:35)
- **Visual:** Briefly open the "Skincare Guidance" modal showing AM/PM steps derived from the scores, and open the "Privacy & Data Control" modal showing 1-click local purge. End on the brand lockup.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "LEKHA pairs these findings with transparent AM/PM skincare considerations, zero cloud photo retention, and complete local privacy. LEKHA: Your skin, documented. Your progress, understood. Thank you."

## Recording Tips for Maximum Hackathon Score
1. **Screen Resolution:** Record at 1080p (1920×1080) at 60 fps or 30 fps using OBS Studio or Loom.
2. **Audio Quality:** Use a clean, quiet environment with a clear microphone.
3. **Pacing:** Keep cursor movements steady and deliberate. Avoid rapid scrolling.
4. **YouTube Settings:** Upload as **Public** (or **Unlisted** if required, but Public is safest for hackathon judges). Title: `LEKHA — Skin Intelligence (Perfect Corp YouCam Hackathon Demo)`. Include the GitHub link and live Vercel URL in the video description.
